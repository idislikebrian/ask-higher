# Ask Higher recruitment readiness

The intake remains opt-in. Do not add people to the directory without a submitted profile and directory-search consent.

## Suggested invitation

> We’re opening a small Ask Higher experiment: an opt-in Higher rolodex for grounded discovery. Add a profile at `/join` with what you work on, what you can help with, what you’re interested in, and what you’re looking for. Your profile is editable, and you can hide or delete it at any time. The information is used to help Higher participants find the right people and projects to talk to. We’re aiming for 15–25 early profiles across the network.

## Initial coverage

Recruit a balanced first group across:

- builders and developers
- product and visual designers
- artists and creative technologists
- organizers and community builders
- project leads
- hardware and physical computing
- community work and research

Target 15–25 opted-in profiles before evaluating real search relevance. Track gaps by capability and project context rather than recruiting only one discipline.
